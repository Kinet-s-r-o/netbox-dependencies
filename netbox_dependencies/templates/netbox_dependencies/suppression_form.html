What suppressing does

While the suppression is active the device is treated as up by the outage analysis:

  • it is not a root cause and is not listed under Live outages,
  • no Teams / Slack / webhook message is sent for it (an outage that was already announced is closed quietly),
  • it does not degrade or take down the devices that depend on it,
  • in the graph and the tree it is shown dimmed with a “Suppressed” mark.

The device keeps being monitored, so its real state is still visible; the suppression ends at the chosen time or when somebody resumes it.
