  1. On the NetBox server run manage.py dependencies_setup_poller --name netbox-poller --netbox-url <address of NetBox> --write dependencies-poller.env — it creates a restricted user, a token and the environment file.

A round is the time to ping every device once. If it takes longer than the interval, rounds simply follow each other without a pause, and when a status gets older than its validity the device turns to “unknown”. The worst round is ceil(devices / at once) × (packets × timeout + (packets − 1) × 0.2 s); in practice most devices answer immediately and rounds are much shorter. The Devices column above shows the same estimate for each poller.

How it works

The poller container asks NetBox what to ping and how, at the start of every round, so changes here apply without restarting it. The environment variables of the container (POLL_INTERVAL, PING_COUNT, …) are only used until NetBox answers.

After every round it reports its heartbeat. If it stays silent longer than the limit a message goes to the notification destinations, and another one when it is back.
