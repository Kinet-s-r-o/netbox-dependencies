How it works

The poller container asks NetBox what to ping and how, at the start of every round, so changes here apply without restarting it. The environment variables of the container (POLL_INTERVAL, PING_COUNT, …) are only used until NetBox answers.

After every round it reports its heartbeat. If it stays silent longer than the limit a message goes to the notification destinations, and another one when it is back.
