Suppressed devices

A suppressed device is taken out of the outage analysis for a while — for example a backup unit that is switched off, or a device in planned maintenance. It is treated as if it were up: it is not a root cause, raises no notification and does not degrade or take down anything. The suppression ends by itself at the chosen time, or when you resume the device.
